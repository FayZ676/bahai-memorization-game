Design Theme
Scripture memorization app

As you learn a line, it fades.

A calm reading surface where scripture recedes into memory. The identity is built on fundamentals — cool paper, one serif for scripture, a single signature color, generous space — and reserves its one meaningful motion for what the app does: as a line is committed to memory, it gently disappears from the page. Every effect is native and cheap; the polish comes from restraint, not spectacle.

01

North star

One deliberate tension, and how it resolves.

Fun

Tactile haptics, springy reveals, a warm streak, encouraging copy — the app should feel alive and rewarding to return to.

Respectful

The scripture itself is never decorated, never animated for spectacle, never reduced to "content." It is set once, cleanly, and left alone.

The resolution: the content stays sacred and still; the chrome is where delight lives. A prayer is set and left alone — while the frame around it (navigation, progress, rewards, transitions) is warm, playful, and alive.

02

Principles

  1. The text is sacred; the chrome is playful. Never decorate scripture. Delight belongs to the frame — motion, haptics, the streak, the copy.
  2. The fade is the signature. Memory made visible through disappearance. It is the one motion that carries meaning; everything else stays quiet.
  3. One serif, one accent, one gold. If everything is special, nothing is. Restraint is the aesthetic.
  4. Space is reverence. Generous margins and line spacing. Let a prayer breathe the way it would on the page of a well-set book.
  5. Reward the return, not the score. Streaks and progress feel warm and human — a candle kept lit, never a leaderboard.
  6. Native and cheap by design. Every effect is stock SwiftUI — opacity, blur, spring, haptics. Polish from fundamentals, not shaders.

The accent — pine on paper, gilt on ink

The accent is one role, two hues — because the two grounds are not mirror images. On paper, pine (#2F6E5B) is a deep teal-green: contemplative, garden-like, and dark enough to carry weight against a bright field. On ink, that same hue has nowhere to go — pushed bright enough to separate from a near-black ground it turns mint, and left dark it sinks. Dark mode instead takes gilt (#C89A5A), whose warmth reads unmistakably as accent against a neutral ground. Either way it is the only accent: one color per ground, used sparingly, is what makes it read as intentional.

Rule: the size chosen in Settings applies as a single uniform multiplier to every text token — serif content and sans chrome alike. Medium is the base sizes shown in the tables above. The multiplier scales text only; icon glyphs, padding, and layout metrics stay fixed.

The serif is Cormorant, reserved for content; the chrome is system sans throughout. Scripture sets at line-height ~1.6 — spacing is where reverence shows.

Rule: the serif ships in four weights — Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold. Bold belongs to headings and titles (heading, passageTitle), where it separates a name from the body beneath it. Scripture itself never sets bolder than Medium; the weight is there to structure the page, not to emphasise the words.

Rule: any and all scripture/prayer text — full body, snippet, preview, or title, in any list row, detail view, or search result — renders in a serif Content token (passageTitle, recite, prayer, verse, numeral). Never a sans chrome token, regardless of where in the app it appears.

Rule: author bylines (who a prayer/passage is attributed to) are metadata, not scripture — they stay in the sans footnote token, never serif. To still read as a quiet attribution rather than plain chrome, they render italic.

A raised surface is a subtle fill + 1px hairline, plus at most one soft shadow in light mode. No heavy drop shadows, and no glows — a calm surface reads as more reverent.

06

The signature — the fade

The one motion that means something. When a card settles, its hidden words fade out in a smooth left-to-right sweep — each dissolving with a touch of blur and a slight rise, on a spring, and a light haptic tick as it lands. As a word clears, a single slim line the width of the word settles into its place — one clean blank, not dotted underscores — cueing you to recite it from memory. The same sweep scales down to a single word, its letters fading left to right with punctuation left standing. You can also hide or show any word yourself — tap it, or glide across a run. Reveal is the same motion, reversed.

Progress spine — an accent ramp by mastery depth

One segment per chunk, so you can still count them. Each fills with accent in proportion to how deeply that chunk is hidden — full for locked-in, a faint tint for not-yet-started. A single-hue ramp — color that deepens with mastery, never a rainbow.

07

Motion & haptics

Motion is reserved for hide/reveal, navigation, and rewards — the page is otherwise still; nothing bobs, floats, or drifts on its own. Haptics work the opposite way: every physical interaction answers with one — every tap, glide, swipe, and scrub lands in the hand. Generous but never noise: one crisp response per action, nothing continuous. Honor Reduce Motion.

11

Paste-ready token block

A compressed version to drop straight into a design prompt.

THEME: calm scripture-memorization app. Reverent + minimal, with playful chrome.
CONCEPT: the sacred text is still and undecorated; all delight lives in the frame
         (haptics, streak, transitions, copy). Signature motion = text fading away as
         it's memorized (opacity+blur+rise, staggered left-to-right). No shaders/glows.
FADE: hidden words fade left-to-right (per word AND per letter); each faded word
      leaves a slim line its own width as a recite cue — one clean blank, never dotted underscores.

MODE: light default, optional dark.

COLOR (light): paper #ECEEE8 · raised #FAFBF7 · ink #1C2521 · muted #6A716B ·
               faint #9DA39C · hairline #DFE2DB · accent(pine) #2F6E5B · gold #B9863F
COLOR (dark):  paper #121212 · raised #1E1E1E · ink #EDEDED · muted #9A9A9A ·
               faint #6A6A6A · hairline rgba(255,255,255,.07) · accent(gilt) #C89A5A · gold #F0CE8A
ACCENT is pine #2F6E5B on light, gilt #C89A5A on dark — one role, two hues, because pine
       cannot separate from a near-black ground. Use ~5% of a screen.
GOLD means reward only — used <1%. On dark it lifts to #F0CE8A to stay clear of gilt.

TYPE:  serif = Cormorant Garamond, ONLY for scripture, prayer titles, streak numeral.
       serif weights = Regular / Medium / SemiBold / Bold. Bold is for headings and
       titles only — scripture body never sets bolder than Medium.
       sans  = SF Pro / system-ui, for ALL interface chrome.
       Labels are Title Case — never all-caps — with modest +.03em tracking. Scripture line-height ~1.6.
       Author bylines stay sans/footnote (they're attribution, not scripture) but render
       italic — a quiet editorial cue, distinct from plain chrome, without crossing into serif.

SHAPE: cards radius 16; buttons/pills fully rounded (999); icon buttons circular.
       Near-flat: raised fill + 1px hairline + at most one soft shadow (light only).
       No glows. Generous whitespace; 4pt spacing grid.

MOTION: reserved for hide/reveal, navigation, rewards. Otherwise still.
        easeInOut 120–280ms; spring(response .3–.42, damping .8). Respect reduce-motion.
HAPTICS: every physical interaction (tap, glide, swipe, scrub) gets one. One per action, nothing continuous.

ICONS: SF Symbols only, referenced by name — never hand-drawn. Approved set:
       chevron.left · chevron.right · chevron.down · ellipsis · gearshape · plus ·
       magnifyingglass · xmark.circle.fill · square.and.pencil · trash ·
       mic · mic.fill · mic.slash · sparkles

VOICE: warm, plain, encouraging. Never gamified-cutesy or preachy.
       Always "prayer"/"passage", never "card"/"content".
