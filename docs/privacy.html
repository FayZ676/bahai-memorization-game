Privacy Policy

Verses · Last updated 8 August 2026

Verses collects nothing about you automatically. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The only information that ever leaves your iPhone is a feedback message, and only if you choose to write one and tap send.

What stays on your device

The passages you add, the words you've hidden, and your daily record of words hidden are saved in the app's own storage on your iPhone. They are never uploaded, shared, or seen by anyone but you.

If you delete Verses, this information is deleted with it.

Microphone and speech

Verses asks for microphone access only so it can follow along while you recite a passage aloud. Speech recognition is performed on your device using Apple's built-in speech framework.

Audio is not recorded, saved, or sent to any server — including mine. It is used momentarily to check the word you just spoke, then discarded. The microphone is active only while you are on the recitation screen and have started reciting.

Feedback you send

Settings has a feedback screen. Nothing is sent from it until you write a message and tap send. When you do, these are submitted:

  • whether you marked it a problem, an idea, or something else;
  • the message you wrote;
  • your email address, only if you choose to fill that field in, so I can write back;
  • the app's version number, your iPhone's model, and its iOS version, so problems are easier to trace.

Nothing else is attached — not your passages, not your progress, not your practice record, and no identifier for your device or your copy of the app.

Feedback is delivered through Google Forms and lands in a private spreadsheet only I can see. Google handles it as their privacy policy describes. Your message is used to fix problems and improve the app, and your email only to reply to you. Neither is sold, shared, or used for advertising. If you'd rather not go through Google, email me directly at the address below instead.

Third parties

Verses contains no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking software. Google Forms receives a feedback message only when you send one, as described above; apart from that, no third party receives information about you through this app.

The app uses one open-source software library for searching the built-in prayer collection. It runs entirely on your device and has no network access.

Children

Verses is suitable for all ages. It collects nothing automatically, from children or anyone else. The feedback screen is the only place any information is sent, and only when someone deliberately writes and sends a message.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. If a future version of Verses ever collected anything beyond what is described above, that would be set out here clearly.
